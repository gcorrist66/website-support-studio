# WSS Content & Territory Policy

The rule that lets monthly geo-content help clients without ever pitting two clients against each other.

## The two rules

**1. Territory exclusivity — one client per industry per market.**
We sign only ONE client per industry within a given service-area/metro. We promise it ("I won't sign your competitor down the street") — it protects the client, justifies a higher price, and eliminates local cannibalization by design.

- Define "market" by the trade's realistic service radius — usually a whole metro for roofers/landscapers/HVAC; tighter (zip clusters) only in very dense cities.
- Two same-industry clients are fine ONLY if their service areas don't materially overlap (e.g., Tampa vs. Orlando). Same metro + same industry = conflict; don't take both.

**2. Unique content per client — always.**
Distance never fixes duplicate content; uniqueness does. Same *topic* across clients is fine; same *text* is never fine.

- Each client's articles are written fresh against THEIR towns, projects, and angle.
- Geo-target each client's actual service cities/suburbs/zips so two clients naturally diverge.

## Enforce it at intake
- Add an **industry + territory** field to every client record at intake.
- Before signing, check for an existing client with the same industry whose service area overlaps. If found → flag the conflict before signing.

## Why
Local search (and AI/GEO citations) rank on proximity to the searcher. Non-overlapping service areas don't compete; same-metro same-industry clients do. Territory exclusivity + unique copy = geo-content is pure upside, zero downside to customers.
