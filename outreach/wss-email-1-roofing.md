## Why this works for this list
- **Roofing-native eye-candy** = "that could be mine," not "nice site for someone else."
- **Estimate-first message** matches what your landing page already promises — email and destination tell one story.
- **"You own it / cancel anytime"** kills the #1 objection (getting locked in) right in the first email.
- **No-site variant** speaks directly to the 29 (the majority) where the pain is sharpest.

## Deliverability + image specs (unchanged)
- One image, hosted, ~600px, under ~150KB, real alt text, whole email under ~100KB, warmed inbox only.
- Confirm click-tracking fires (the flat 0% across all Zoho campaigns is still unverified).
