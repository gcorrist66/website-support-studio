## Screening Standard

WSS is for small owner-operated or family-operated local service businesses where the website is neglected and maintenance is the pain point.

The right promise is:

> We build it. We host it. We maintain it. We update it. You run your business.

This screen removes companies where the real opportunity is SEO, PPC, conversion strategy, location-page strategy, mature lead generation, or complex operations.
