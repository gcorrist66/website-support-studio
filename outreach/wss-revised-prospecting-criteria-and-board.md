Generated: 2026-06-17

Mission: stop targeting businesses where a redesign could be perceived as a risk to existing SEO value. WSS should target obvious, low-risk refresh wins for owner-operated local businesses.

## New WSS ICP

WSS should pursue owner-operated local businesses that look under-served by their current website, not businesses already benefiting from an active SEO/content machine.

Ideal WSS signals:

- 1-10 employees or owner/operator feel.
- 1-10 meaningful pages.
- No site, broken site, thin site, old design, or neglected mobile experience.
- Minimal service-area footprint.
- No visible content strategy.
- No obvious agency-built SEO structure.
- No or minimal tracking/content/landing-page machinery.
- Owner likely wears multiple hats and can immediately understand the value of a refresh.

Disqualifying signals:

- Large sitemap, many internal links, or location/service-area architecture.
- Blog/news/content program, even if thin.
- Strong schema/tracking/SEO plugin footprint combined with many service/location pages.
- Multi-location or broad service-area operation.
- Sophisticated brand, financing/warranty funnels, legal/solar/high-ticket consultation funnels.
- Any business likely to think, "Do not break our rankings."

## Review Method

I reviewed the current Top 50 from the WSS revenue-board reclassification and ran a lightweight live-site check where URLs were available:

- homepage availability
- homepage internal-link count
- sitemap URL count when available
- platform hints
- blog/news/content signals
- location/service-area signals
- tracking/schema/SEO sophistication signals
- offer/financing/warranty/high-ticket complexity

This is not a paid keyword-count audit. It is a practical WSS risk screen: if a prospect has signs of meaningful SEO equity, content architecture, or marketing maturity, it leaves WSS.

## New Prospecting Rule

Before adding any future prospect to WSS, run this fast gate:

1. Does the business appear owner-operated and local?
2. Is the site missing, broken, stale, or clearly under-built?
3. Are there fewer than roughly 10 meaningful pages?
4. Is there no obvious active blog, location-page, or service-area SEO program?
5. Would a non-technical owner immediately see the redesign as an upgrade rather than a risk?

If any answer is "no," do not put the prospect in WSS. Route it to Corriston Consulting or Enterprise.
