# WSS Website Health Scorecard

**Working title:** *Is Your Website Winning You Jobs? — Score it in 2 minutes.*
**Goal:** a value-first front door that (1) qualifies local service businesses, (2) captures contact + self-reported pain, and (3) routes low/mid scorers into the free-preview offer.
**Use it as:** a standalone scorecard campaign, the CTA on the demo sites/landing pages, and/or the link inside the email drip (swap "get your free preview" for "score your website").

---

## Intro screen (before questions)
> **How healthy is your website — really?**
> Answer 9 quick questions and get a free, personalized Website Health Report: your score, what's costing you calls, and the 3 fastest fixes. Takes 2 minutes. No cost.
> *(For {{vertical}} businesses.)*

---

## The questions (9) — each answer is scored 0 / 5 / 10

**Q1. Do you have a website right now?**
- No — just a Facebook page or Google listing — **0**
- Yes, but it's old or I'm not sure it's working — **5**
- Yes, a modern site — **10**

**Q2. When was it last updated?**
- Over 3 years ago, or not sure — **0**
- In the last 1–3 years — **5**
- Within the last year — **10**

**Q3. On a phone, how does it look and work?**
- Hard to use, or I've never checked — **0**
- Okay, but clunky — **5**
- Clean and easy — **10**

**Q4. Can a customer request a quote or book service right from the site?**
- No — **0**
- Only a basic contact form or phone number — **5**
- Yes — an obvious quote/booking button — **10**

**Q5. Does it show reviews, real photos of your work, and proof you're licensed/insured?**
- None of these — **0**
- Some — **5**
- Yes, front and center — **10**

**Q6. In a typical month, how many new customers come from your website?**
- Zero, or I don't know — **0**
- A few — **5**
- A steady stream — **10**

**Q7. How fast does it load?**
- Slow, or not sure — **0**
- Okay — **5**
- Fast — **10**

**Q8. When someone searches "{{vertical}} near me," does your business show up?**
- No, or not sure — **0**
- Sometimes — **5**
- Usually near the top — **10**

**Q9. Do you fully own your domain, website, and content — could you move it anytime?**
- No, or not sure — **0**
- I think so — **5**
- Yes, completely — **10**

**Max score: 90.**

## Result tiers + messaging

**0–30 — "At Risk: your website is costing you jobs."**
> Right now your site is working against you — customers can't easily find you, trust you, or contact you, so those jobs go to the competitor whose site does. The good news: this is very fixable.
> **CTA → "See a free preview of what your site could look like."** *(Hot WSS buyer.)*

**31–60 — "Needs Work: you're leaving calls on the table."**
> You've got a presence, but it's not pulling its weight — a few key gaps (mobile, quote button, reviews, or speed) are quietly costing you leads. Closing them is straightforward.
> **CTA → "Get your free preview and see the difference."** *(Strong WSS buyer.)*

**61–90 — "Strong — but there's room to convert more."**
> Your site is in good shape. The opportunity now is staying current and squeezing more leads out of the traffic you already get — which is exactly what we handle so you don't have to.
> **CTA → "See how we keep sites like yours current and converting."** *(Maintenance/recurring or content upsell.)*

## How it plugs into the machine
- **The 9 answers ARE the personalization fuel.** Each low answer maps to a WSS service (no mobile → responsive rebuild; no quote button → estimate-first design; no reviews → trust section; not found in search → the SEO/GEO content). Follow-ups reference *what they told you*, not a guess.
- **Routing:** scores 0–60 → free-preview offer + (ideally) a pre-built preview. 61–90 → maintenance/content offer.
- **Email drip swap:** subject *"how does {{company}}'s website score?"* → link to the scorecard instead of the preview. The report becomes the warm bridge to the offer.
- **Per vertical:** swap `{{vertical}}` (roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, pools, irrigation). Questions stay the same; the search question (Q8) and result imagery localize per trade.

## Build vs. buy
- **ScoreApp:** paste these questions + scoring + result copy; it gives you hosting, the gated report, and CRM sync to Apollo. Live today.
- **Roll your own:** Codex builds the scorecard page + scoring, Claude generates a personalized report per respondent from their 9 answers, results pipe to Apollo. More defensible, reusable, and itself a sellable service.
