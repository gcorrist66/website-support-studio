# WSS Preview Acceptance Checklist

**Required before any preview is marked complete.**

## 1) Recognition Test (required gate)

- [ ] Would the owner recognize their company?
- [ ] Are at least **3 identity assets** preserved?
- [ ] Is business name/legal name/industry language present and correct?
- [ ] Are logo/brand assets and messaging non-WSS?

## 2) Improvement Test

- [ ] Is conversion better than prior baseline?
- [ ] Is content hierarchy better than prior baseline?
- [ ] Is mobile experience better than prior baseline?
- [ ] Is there a keep vs improve matrix with rationale?

## 3) System Test

- [ ] Is the assigned design system clearly readable throughout the page?
- [ ] Is any conflicting generic WSS chrome removed from prospect-facing sections?
- [ ] Does the page reflect the assigned system-specific closing section and structure?

## 4) Identity & Trust Signals

- [ ] Does the page preserve business identity assets over system styling?
- [ ] Are trust signals (reviews, proof, local credibility, etc.) present where required by system intent?
- [ ] Are real business details shown before placeholders?

## 5) QA Evidence Required

- [ ] Before screenshots attached
- [ ] After screenshots attached
- [ ] Full-page grayscale screenshots attached (desktop + mobile)
- [ ] Desktop QA screenshots attached
- [ ] Mobile QA screenshots attached
- [ ] Metadata/JSON-LD evidence captured (if changed)

## Pass/Fail Rule

- If any item in this checklist fails, the preview is **NO-GO**.
- A preview is **complete** only when all checklist items are marked **PASS**.
