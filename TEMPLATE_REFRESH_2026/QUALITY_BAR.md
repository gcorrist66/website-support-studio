# WSS 2026 Template Quality Bar

Derived from the current quality leaders: **Florida Boys Lawn & Landscape, Rose Garage Door, Eastside Garage Door, All Trees Considered, Construction Theory.**

This is the standard every public WSS sample template should meet so it reads like a legitimate **$500 setup + ~$200/mo managed service**.

> Fictional-sample rule: sample templates must NOT fabricate testimonials, ratings, review counts, certifications, or results. Credibility is built from real structure (clarity, completeness, local specificity, professional polish) and clearly-labeled stock imagery — never fake social proof.

## 1. Hero
- Large, confident, benefit-led **H1** (desktop max ~2.8–3.6rem, fluid `clamp`). Never a small headline.
- Eyebrow trust-pill (system/locale or "owner-operated since…") above the H1.
- One-paragraph lead that names the offer + place + who you're dealing with.
- **Dual CTA**: one strong primary (the main action) + one secondary; a quiet tertiary at most.
- A credibility aside or 3-item trust row with icons (licensed/insured/years, or hours/location/booking for retail).
- Real or tasteful stock imagery with a caption; legible text via overlay where text sits on image.

## 2. Navigation
- Sticky header: logo + links + a prominent primary CTA; phone surfaced for service businesses.
- **Working mobile menu (hamburger → drawer).** Hiding nav with `display:none` on mobile is a defect.

## 3. CTA hierarchy
- One clearly dominant primary action, repeated down the page and in a closing band.
- Service businesses: phone reachable in header and in a **sticky mobile bar** (Call + primary action).
- Don't dilute with 4 competing CTAs of equal weight.

## 4. Trust presentation
- Icon-backed checkmark strip / chips, not just plain text bullets.
- Local specificity (service area, neighborhoods, hours, address).
- For service trades: licensed/insured/years where real; for retail/hospitality: hours, location, booking/visit path.

## 5. Reviews / social proof
- Real sites: rating summary block + individual review cards.
- Fictional samples: NO fabricated reviews. Substitute honest credibility (process clarity, completeness, "what makes it sales-ready"), and say it's a sample.

## 6. Gallery / proof
- Captioned **image** grid of work / products / space — not text-in-a-gradient-box placeholders.
- Service/product/menu cards: titled, with meta (price/duration) where relevant.

## 7. Owner / story
- Short narrative (who runs it, how they work) + a checklist + a CTA. Humanizes the business.

## 8. Final CTA band + footer
- Bold closing band with the primary action.
- Multi-column footer (brand/contact + nav), with phone/email/address and (for real sites) socials.

## 9. Mobile polish & perceived value
- No horizontal overflow; fluid type via `clamp`; tap targets ≥44px.
- Sticky mobile action bar; working hamburger nav.
- Generous spacing, consistent palette, subtle shadows/borders → looks premium and believable.

## Quick scorecard (per template)
Hero · Nav (+mobile) · CTA hierarchy · Trust · Reviews/credibility · Gallery/proof · Owner story · Final CTA · Footer · Mobile · Perceived value → **A keep / B refresh / C rebuild.**
