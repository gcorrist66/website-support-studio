Ideal customer profiles
WSS — Scale ICP
PE-backed or owner-operated multi-location home service groups managing 3+ brands or 5+ locations. CMO, Director of Marketing, or President. Pain: maintaining consistent web presence across all locations without in-house dev. Segment: Home Services Scale.
CBO — Agency ICP
Agency owner or Head of Paid Media managing $50K+/month in multi-platform ad spend (Google + Meta + at minimum one other platform). Pain: making cross-platform budget decisions without a unified intelligence layer. Segment: Agencies.
CBO — In-House ICP
VP Marketing or SVP Marketing at a mid-market ecommerce or SaaS company with $2M+/year ad spend across 3+ platforms. Pain: platform reps each claim credit, no source-of-truth for where the next dollar goes. Segment: Ecommerce / B2B SaaS.
IntrynSync ICP
Marketing Ops Manager, RevOps Director, or Fractional RevOps consultant managing 4+ integrated SaaS tools (Marketo/HubSpot + Salesforce + any data warehouse). Pain: data sync failures, workflow orchestration gaps, AI integrations that break on schema change. Segment: RevOps / MarOps.
ConversionHealth ICP
Agency running paid media + CRO together, or in-house marketing director at a company where AI optimization tools are making decisions on degraded conversion signals. Pain: GA4 / pixel drift / iOS changes / third-party cookie deprecation eroding signal confidence without anyone auditing it systematically. Segment: Agencies / Ecommerce / B2B SaaS.
