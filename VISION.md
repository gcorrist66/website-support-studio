# WSS Vision

## Vision Statement
Enable a reliable, auditable, and tenant-isolated website support operating model where customer support requests move from intake to resolution with human accountability at every decision point.

## Why now
Teams currently lack a clearly defined operating model for website support that separates execution from governance while keeping governance lightweight enough for small teams and scalable enough for future multi-tenancy.

## What WSS will be
A disciplined support platform with:
- A clear organizational hierarchy: **Agency → Client → Site → Ticket**.
- Standardized MMVP workflow with clear approval checkpoints.
- Strong governance hooks through IntrynSync.
- Execution leverage from HiveRunner.

## What WSS will not be
- It is not a rebrand or clone of existing internal products.
- It is not an autonomous AI support replacement.
- It is not a data platform, ticketing SaaS, or CRM.
- It is not an internal agent that creates or edits production systems in phase one.
